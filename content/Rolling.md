**Roll the appropriately sided die according to what you noted down during [[Character creation]] and add whatever bonuses apply for the situation.**

**To pass the check, your total result must be higher than the difficulty check (DC). If the result >= DC+10 then it is considered a critical success.**

**If a dice lands on it's highest numbered face, it explodes. You may roll again and add the next result to the total. There is no limit to how many times this can repeat.**

**If you are under no pressure to complete a task, you may take the average of your die as the roll result. If this isn't enough to pass the DC, you will still succeed after some amount of time.**
