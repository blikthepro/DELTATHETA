This one is gonna be the most open ended action.

ACTing lets you do something to convince the enemy to stop fighting. Most of the time it will just be a simple skill roll, other times it may be more complicated.
Successful ACT rolls raise the enemy's <span style="color:yellow"> Mercy </span> score by some amount, unsuccessful rolls only raise it by half of that amount, minimum of 1.
If there are multiple enemies of the same type, an ACT targeting one of them will affect the others too.

You always have the following ACT available:

`Check[1AP]`: analyze the enemy, provide potential ACTs.


You do not need to Check the enemy to be able to use an ACT against them, it just provides pointers. You can always be creative and come up with your own ACTs
