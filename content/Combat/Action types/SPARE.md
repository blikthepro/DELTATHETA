Enemies have a <span style="color:yellow"> Mercy </span> score, ranging from 1 to 10.

[[ACT]] s raise the score of whichever enemy was targeted by the [[ACT]].

Once the <span style="color:yellow"> Mercy </span> score reaches 10, the enemy can be spared. They immediately and peacefully leave the battle.

Trying to SPARE an enemy with a <span style="color:yellow"> Mercy </span> score below 10 just increases their score by 1.
