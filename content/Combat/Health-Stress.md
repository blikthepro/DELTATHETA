**Any time you take damage during combat, you may apply it either to <span style="color:red"> HP </span> or <span style="color:orange"> STR </span>**
# **<span style="color:red"> HP </span>**
**Physical status of your body, or a measurement of your metaphysical will to keep fighting, who knows. Damage reduces <span style="color:red"> HP </span>**

**If your <span style="color:red"> HP </span> reaches 0, you are DOWNed.** 
- **You cannot perform any actions.** 
- **Your <span style="color:red"> HP </span> is set to -5.**
- **While DOWNed, you regenerate 1 <span style="color:red"> HP </span> per round.** 
- **If your <span style="color:red"> HP </span> reaches a positive value (either by healing magic, ITEMs, or the aforementioned regeneration), you are brought back up, and can continue fighting.**

**True death only happens if all party members are DOWNed.**

# **<span style="color:orange">STR</span>**

**Your general mental state, your ability to not mess up. Damage increases <span style="color:orange"> STR </span>.**


**Several things can increase or decrease <span style="color:orange"> STR </span> over the span of combat.** 
- **Things may increase <span style="color:orange"> STR </span> temporarily, such as casting a spell. Temporary <span style="color:orange"> STR </span> gained this way is removed at the end of a round.**
- **Things may increase <span style="color:orange"> STR </span> for longer periods of time, such as a concentration spell. <span style="color:orange"> STR </span> gained this way is removed when the effect producing it ends.**
- **<span style="color:orange"> STR </span> gained from damage decreases by 5 if you defeat or spare foes, and by 1 at the end of a round.**
**If damage received exceeds your <span style="color:orange"> STR </span> capacity, you become stunned:**
- **Whatever action you had planned, if it hasn't already happened, gets cancelled.**
