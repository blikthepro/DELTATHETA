**Every combat round in DELTATHETA lasts 1 second. There is no turn order. Instead, the players pick someone to take their turn, then the gm picks one of the enemies, repeat until everyone has taken turns it and then comes the next round.**
**If one side can take more turns than the other, they take them at the end of the round.**

Whenever 2 creatures are 2 units away from each other, try to resolve their actions as if they happen at the same time
